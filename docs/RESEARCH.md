# Market Research

**Gathered: 16 August 2026. This market moves 
monthly. Treat anything here older than about 
three months as needing re-verification before 
it informs a decision.**

All figures are as advertised by the provider at 
time of search. List prices and discounted prices 
were often shown together, which suggests permanent 
discounting is normal in this segment. Currency is 
as stated by the source, mostly USD.

---

## 4. The direct competitor worth watching

**iso42001toolkit.com** runs the following funnel:

1. Free readiness assessment, two minutes, scores 
   across nine governance domains, no signup required
2. Produces a board-ready report
3. Funnels into a paid documentation toolkit 
   from USD 199

They also advertise EU AI Act and Microsoft SSPA 
alignment as differentiators, and cite specific 
regulatory dates as selling points. Those dates 
were stated on their site and have **not** been 
independently verified. Do not repeat any 
regulatory date from a competitor's marketing 
without checking it against a primary source.

**What this means for us:** free assessment into 
paid product is not a differentiator. Someone else 
built it first and did it well. Our differentiator 
has to be tone, structure, and the board 
communication layer, or it does not exist.

---

## 5. Where the gap actually is

Nothing found in this research serves the 
non-specialist. Free courses assume comfort with 
standards language. Toolkits assume you already 
know what you are building and need the documents. 
Accredited training is for people becoming 
specialists.

The person who was handed AI responsibility three 
months ago, has no compliance background, does not 
know what AIMS stands for, and has a board meeting 
coming is not being served by anyone found in 
this search.

That is the thesis. It has not been validated with 
a single buyer yet.
